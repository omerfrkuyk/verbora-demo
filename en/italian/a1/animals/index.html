Questions and answers

How many words are on this list?

The table has 27 words. By level: A1: 24, B1: 3.

How many of these nouns are masculine or feminine?

The 27 nouns on this list: masculine 17, feminine 10. Articles and adjectives follow the gender, so learn each noun with it.

How do you know the gender of an Italian noun?

Italian nouns ending in -o are mostly masculine (il libro) and those ending in -a mostly feminine (la casa); nouns ending in -e can be either (il pane, la notte). Before a vowel the article is l', so these lists show the gender separately.
