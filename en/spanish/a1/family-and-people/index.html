Questions and answers

How many words are on this list?

The table has 59 words. By level: A1: 34, A2: 25.

How many of these nouns are masculine or feminine?

The 53 nouns on this list: masculine 35, feminine 17, the same for both 1. Articles and adjectives follow the gender, so learn each noun with it.

How do you know the gender of a Spanish noun?

Spanish nouns ending in -o are mostly masculine (el libro) and those ending in -a mostly feminine (la casa), with exceptions such as el día, el mapa and la mano. Feminine nouns that start with a stressed a take el in the singular: el agua is feminine (las aguas). That is why these lists show the gender separately.
