Questions and answers

How was the frequency worked out?

The ranking follows the OpenSubtitles 2018 frequency list built from film and TV subtitles (FrequencyWords, CC BY-SA 4.0). Subtitles are close to everyday speech, so the list puts the words you hear most in spoken language first.

Why are words like "I", "and" and "a" missing?

Verbora's lists teach only nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs and numbers. Pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions and articles do not fit a puzzle or a picture card, so they are not in this list either; they are learned inside sentences.

Which levels are these 100 words?

By level: A1: 89, A2: 11. Most of the most common words are taught at the beginner levels.
