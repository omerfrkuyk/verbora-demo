    Questions and answers

    What is on this page?

    20 phrases for directions and getting around (Asking the way, Metro, bus and tram, Taxis, Hiring a car) and 28 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in French

    Use vous with strangers and anyone serving you: "Vous pouvez m'aider ?". Use tu with friends and family. In France, saying "Bonjour" before your first question in a shop or to a stranger is part of being polite.

    Does it work without internet?
