    Questions and answers

    What is on this page?

    25 phrases for restaurant and café (Booking and arriving, Ordering, Allergies and special requests, During the meal, The bill) and 28 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in French

    Use vous with strangers and anyone serving you: "Vous pouvez m'aider ?". Use tu with friends and family. In France, saying "Bonjour" before your first question in a shop or to a stranger is part of being polite.
