    What is on this page?

    23 phrases for doctor and pharmacy (At the pharmacy, Describing symptoms, At the doctor's, Insurance and paying) and 26 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in German

    Use Sie with strangers, waiters, doctors and the police: "Können Sie mir helfen?". Use du with friends, family and children. If in doubt, say Sie; switch when the other person offers du.
