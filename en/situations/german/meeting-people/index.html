    Questions and answers

    What is on this page?

    25 phrases for meeting people and small talk (Hello and goodbye, Introducing yourself, Small talk, Making plans, When you don't understand) and 25 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in German

    Use Sie with strangers, waiters, doctors and the police: "Können Sie mir helfen?". Use du with friends, family and children. If in doubt, say Sie; switch when the other person offers du.

    Does it work without internet?

    Yes. Once you open this section, all the situation pages of that language are saved on your phone, so they open abroad even without internet. You can also add it to your home screen and use it like an app. The saved pages stay on your device only.
