Questions and answers

Which situations are there?

Travel and the airport (25 phrases), Hotel (23 phrases), Restaurant and café (25 phrases), Shopping and bargaining (25 phrases), Directions and getting around (21 phrases), Doctor and pharmacy (23 phrases), Meeting people and small talk (25 phrases), Emergencies (23 phrases).

Formal and casual address in German

Use Sie with strangers, waiters, doctors and the police: "Können Sie mir helfen?". Use du with friends, family and children. If in doubt, say Sie; switch when the other person offers du.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once you open this section, all the situation pages of that language are saved on your phone, so they open abroad even without internet. You can also add it to your home screen and use it like an app. The saved pages stay on your device only.

How were these phrases made?

The situations and phrases come from one shared list for five languages and were written separately in each language, then checked twice for naturalness and accuracy. They were prepared with the help of AI and have not yet been read by a native speaker: if you spot a mistake, tell us.
