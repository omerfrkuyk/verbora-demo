    Questions and answers

    What is on this page?

    25 phrases for shopping and bargaining (Looking around and prices, Sizes and trying on, Bargaining at the market, Paying, Returns and exchanges) and 27 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in Spanish

    Usted is the polite, formal form; in Spain tú is also very common, even with young staff. If in doubt use usted and switch if the other person uses tú. In Latin America usted is heard more often.
