    Questions and answers

    What is on this page?

    23 phrases for emergencies (Calling for help, Making yourself understood, Accidents and injuries, Theft and the police, Lost documents) and 27 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in Spanish

    Usted is the polite, formal form; in Spain tú is also very common, even with young staff. If in doubt use usted and switch if the other person uses tú. In Latin America usted is heard more often.

    Does it work without internet?

    Yes. Once you open this section, all the situation pages of that language are saved on your phone, so they open abroad even without internet. You can also add it to your home screen and use it like an app. The saved pages stay on your device only.
