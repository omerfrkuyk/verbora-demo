    What is on this page?

    24 phrases for travel and the airport (Check-in and bags, Passport control and customs, At the gate and on board, Arrival and baggage problems, Train and coach stations) and 26 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in Spanish

    Usted is the polite, formal form; in Spain tú is also very common, even with young staff. If in doubt use usted and switch if the other person uses tú. In Latin America usted is heard more often.
