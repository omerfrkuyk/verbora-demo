    Questions and answers

    What is on this page?

    21 phrases for directions and getting around (Asking the way, Metro, bus and tram, Taxis, Hiring a car) and 28 key words. Every phrase has its meaning in English; some have a short note on formal or casual use, an article or a local habit.

    Formal and casual address in Italian

    Use Lei with strangers, waiters and doctors, with the verb in the third person: "Può aiutarmi?". Use tu with friends and children. To get someone's attention, say "Scusi" (formal) or "Scusa" (casual).
