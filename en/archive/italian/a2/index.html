Questions and answers

How many words are there at Italian A2?

Verbora's Italian A2 list has 377 words: 216 nouns, 76 verbs, 70 adjectives and 15 adverbs. Small words such as pronouns, prepositions and conjunctions are not counted; exam lists count them too, so the numbers cannot be compared one to one.

How was the level of each word set?

Each word's level follows De Mauro's Nuovo vocabolario di base and the Kelly list; every word was also looked at with a teacher's eye, and the harder of the two judgements was kept. The lists are drafts: a native speaker is still reading them.

How do you know the gender of an Italian noun?

Italian nouns ending in -o are mostly masculine (il libro) and those ending in -a mostly feminine (la casa); nouns ending in -e can be either (il pane, la notte). Before a vowel the article is l', so these lists show the gender separately.
