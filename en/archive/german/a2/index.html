Questions and answers

How many words are there at German A2?

Verbora's German A2 list has 381 words: 219 nouns, 78 verbs, 67 adjectives and 17 adverbs. Small words such as pronouns, prepositions and conjunctions are not counted; exam lists count them too, so the numbers cannot be compared one to one.

How was the level of each word set?

Each word's level follows the Goethe-Institut B1 word list and DAFlex; every word was also looked at with a teacher's eye, and the harder of the two judgements was kept. The lists are drafts: a native speaker is still reading them.

How do you learn German articles?

Every German noun has its own article (der, die, das) and no rule always works. Some endings help: nouns ending in -ung, -heit, -keit and -schaft take die, those ending in -chen and -lein take das, and days, months and seasons take der. The easiest way is to learn every noun with its article: der Hund, die Katze, das Haus.
