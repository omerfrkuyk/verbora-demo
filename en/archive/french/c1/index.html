Questions and answers

How many words are there at French C1?

Verbora's French C1 list has 274 words: 81 nouns, 140 verbs and 53 adjectives. Small words such as pronouns, prepositions and conjunctions are not counted; exam lists count them too, so the numbers cannot be compared one to one.

How was the level of each word set?

Each word's level follows FLELex; every word was also looked at with a teacher's eye, and the harder of the two judgements was kept. The lists are drafts: a native speaker is still reading them.

How do you know the gender of a French noun?

Every French noun is masculine (le) or feminine (la). Before a vowel or a silent h both become l', so these lists show the gender separately. Nouns ending in -tion, -sion and -té are mostly feminine, those ending in -ment and -age mostly masculine, with exceptions (la page, la plage).
