Questions and answers

How many words are there at Spanish B2?

Verbora's Spanish B2 list has 465 words: 236 nouns, 111 verbs, 117 adjectives and 1 adverb. Small words such as pronouns, prepositions and conjunctions are not counted; exam lists count them too, so the numbers cannot be compared one to one.

How was the level of each word set?

Each word's level follows the Instituto Cervantes curriculum (PCIC) and ELELex; every word was also looked at with a teacher's eye, and the harder of the two judgements was kept. The lists are drafts: a native speaker is still reading them.

How do you know the gender of a Spanish noun?

Spanish nouns ending in -o are mostly masculine (el libro) and those ending in -a mostly feminine (la casa), with exceptions such as el día, el mapa and la mano. Feminine nouns that start with a stressed a take el in the singular: el agua is feminine (las aguas). That is why these lists show the gender separately.
