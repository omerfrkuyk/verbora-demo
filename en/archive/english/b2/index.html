Questions and answers

How many words are there at English B2?

Verbora's English B2 list has 397 words: 206 nouns, 78 verbs and 113 adjectives. Small words such as pronouns, prepositions and conjunctions are not counted; exam lists count them too, so the numbers cannot be compared one to one.

How was the level of each word set?

Each word's level follows the Oxford 3000 and Oxford 5000 lists; every word was also looked at with a teacher's eye, and the harder of the two judgements was kept. The lists are drafts: a native speaker is still reading them.

Does English have articles and gender?

English nouns have no grammatical gender; the definite article is always the. The indefinite article a becomes an before a vowel sound: a book, an apple. That is why the English lists show no article.
