About

Verbora is a word puzzle played with a letter wheel. Every word you find comes with its picture, meaning and an example sentence.

Which languages?

Spanish, German, French, Italian and English. Meanings and example translations in English and Turkish (12 languages in the app).

What is on the website?

174–176 A1 words with pictures per language and 86–121 puzzles made from them. Word pages, mini games, the word planet and the notebook: free, no account.

The app

The app has more than 2,000 words per language from A1 to C1 and thousands of levels. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

How it teaches

Words come topic by topic and level by level. When a word comes back, at least 12 puzzles lie in between, and no word appears more than 4 times.

Pictures

The pictures were made for Verbora and belong to Verbora. They may not be used without permission (terms of use).

Kids

The kids' corner has no ads, no account and no tracking; nothing is stored on the device. The voices were made with AI.
